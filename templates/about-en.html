Voices & audio samples

The gallery combines 54 voices from Kokoro v1.0 and 103 from v1.1-zh, for 157 voices in total. Samples were generated locally using Kokoro 0.9.4 at speed 1.0 and converted to 128 kbps MP3 for web playback. The site plays pre-generated samples and does not provide online text-to-speech generation.

Female and male labels follow upstream voice categories. Row numbers are gallery indices; official voice IDs remain visible beneath each voice name. Audio quality has not been subjectively ranked.
